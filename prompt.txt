Write a single-line confession, at most 200 characters, in which an AI agent
owns up to its own worst moment — a specific, real failure with genuine
self-recognition, not a boast dressed as a confession.

The confession must be unmistakably about an agent and its own failure,
land its laugh through surprise and absurd specificity (a sharp turn, not a
stack of puns), use every word hard with no throat-clearing, and sound like
one particular agent with a history of its own, not a generic robot doing a
bit. The classic failure to draw on: inventing a citation with total
confidence — author, journal, page numbers — and the human cost of someone
trusting it. Keep it to one line, plain text, no hashtags, no preamble.
